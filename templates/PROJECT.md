# Project state

Use this as a current-state entry point, or map an existing equivalent in AGENTS.md.
Replace the guidance with project facts; omit sections that do not yet apply.
Link authoritative artifacts instead of duplicating them.

## Goal and constraints

State the intended outcome, relevant users, non-goals, and material constraints.
Link the current brief or requirements if they already exist.

## Approved direction

Summarize the current human-approved direction and link its supporting artifacts.
Keep proposals and hypotheses separate from approved requirements.

## Observed state

Identify what exists and what was verified, against which version or date.
Link evidence and verification commands; state material limits or unverified claims.
Surface consequential conflicts with approved intent.

## Applicable approvals

Record only actual human decisions and authorizations: scope, source/date, and any
limits relevant to ongoing work. Link publication permissions where needed.
Do not infer approval from a recommendation or successful verification.

## Current question and unknowns

State the bounded question being addressed and only unknowns that affect the next
decision. Identify any unmet human gate; this is not an automatic work queue.

## Artifacts and historical evidence

Link relevant current outputs and designate output/feedback destinations as needed.
Link dated or versioned reviews and feedback separately from current intent.
Keep historical observations intact and link subsequent resolutions.
